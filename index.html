Questions

We already have a TMS. Why switch?

Most TMS programs show where a load is after someone types it in. LanePing reads the rate con for you and watches every load against its appointment with live GPS, then carries it through to the invoice and the driver's statement.

Is our data private?

Every company gets its own separate system. Records and documents open only for the people you authorize, and are kept until you delete them.

Which ELDs work?

Samsara and Synergy ELD today, side by side in the same fleet. Tell us yours when you book a demo and we'll confirm the timeline.

Can we start with tracking only?

Yes. Start on Tracking and move to the Full TMS whenever you're ready. Your loads and history come with you.
